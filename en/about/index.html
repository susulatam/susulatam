What is SUSU

SUSU is Latin America's Sustainability Summit.

Not quite yet. We're building it, and we'd rather tell it this way: step by step, in plain sight.

Why Latin America

The region has the assets. What it lacks is the meeting place.

Around 60% of the world's terrestrial, marine and freshwater life is found in Latin America and the Caribbean, according to the UN Environment Programme. Six of the world's seventeen megadiverse countries are here.

And it isn't only nature: 67% of the electricity the region generates already comes from clean sources, according to OLACDE's 2025 Energy Outlook. It's one of the cleanest power grids on the planet.

With numbers like those, the big sustainability conversations still take place in Europe and the United States. We provide the ecosystems, the energy and much of the food, but the debate happens elsewhere. SUSU exists to change that.

The road

Four formats, one destination.

01

SUSU Conversations

Private tables of up to fifteen people, one industry each. This is where we are today.

02

SUSU Roundtables

Working sessions on a concrete challenge, with an outcome at the end.

03

SUSU Sessions

Talks and workshops open to a wider audience, in different cities.

04

SUSU Summit

The big one: conferences, talks, stands and the whole region in one place.

We won't skip steps. Each format exists once the community that sustains it exists.

We don't promise to change the region. We promise to build the place where those who are changing it can meet.
