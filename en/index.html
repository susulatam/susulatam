We're building Latin America's Sustainability Summit.

One gathering at a time. We start with small, curated tables, and we're heading toward the event the region still doesn't have: talks, conferences and spaces where Latin American sustainability shows itself to the world.

Where we are today

Community first. The stage comes later.

SUSU begins with SUSU Conversations: private gatherings of no more than fifteen people, with a single representative per industry. Energy, chemicals, beverages, agtech, engineering, software, consulting. People who don't usually cross paths, sitting at the same table.

That's the foundation. A summit without a community behind it is a rented venue; with one, it's an institution. That's why we start here.
