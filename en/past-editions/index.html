SUSU Conversations #2

Costanera Sur Ecological Reserve, Buenos Aires · October 7, 2026

DateOctober 7, 2026
PlaceCostanera Sur Ecological Reserve
Participants11 people
Industries8 different sectors

Better to do a little than to say a lot.

SUSU's second table took place at the Costanera Sur Ecological Reserve: eleven people, eight industries, chairs in a circle and a wetland all around. The venue was secured by a participant from the first gathering.

We opened again with the photo game and, for the second time, without anyone suggesting it, the conversation landed in the same place: we need to educate, we need to pass it on, and it's better to do a little than to say a lot.

Perspectives that rarely share a table came together: small businesses, corporations, independent consultants and associations. A brewery, a composting company, football, a mobility company, a certification firm, a tech company and a carbon company. Before the coffee was finished there were already connections and ideas for joint initiatives.

Very different perspectives found common ground. That's what this is all about.

First edition

SUSU Conversations #1

Tigre, Buenos Aires · September 2, 2026

DateSeptember 2, 2026
PlaceTigre, Buenos Aires
Participants8 people
Industries6 different sectors

Three hours of conversation, no stage.

The first gathering brought together eight people from six different industries around one table. Energy, chemicals, engineering, agriculture and carbon markets, software development and sustainability consulting. One representative per sector, exactly as planned.

The conversation started with a photo game and ended three hours later without anyone checking the clock. Topics came up that were on no agenda: how to build software with environmental criteria, what the chemical industry is struggling to solve, where a company's responsibility over its value chain ends, and how to tell a company that genuinely measures from one that simply reports well.

Two points came up repeatedly without anyone proposing them. First: nobody knows for certain what the future will look like, but that isn't enough of an excuse not to act. Second: we need to keep connecting and educating, because a huge part of the work ahead is getting this conversation to more people.

Sustainability can't be a topic you deal with once a year. It has to be on the table all the time.
