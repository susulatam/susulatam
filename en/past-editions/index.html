SUSU Conversations #1

Tigre, Buenos Aires · September 2, 2026. The first time the SUSU LATAM table sat down together.

DateSeptember 2, 2026
PlaceTigre, Buenos Aires
Participants8 people
Industries7 different sectors
How it went

Three hours of conversation, no stage.

The first gathering brought together eight people from seven different industries around one table. Energy, chemicals, engineering, agriculture and carbon markets, software development and sustainability consulting. One representative per sector, exactly as planned.

The conversation started with a photo game and ended three hours later without anyone checking the clock. Topics came up that were on no agenda: how to build software with environmental criteria, what the chemical industry is struggling to solve, where a company's responsibility over its value chain ends, and how to tell a company that genuinely measures from one that simply reports well.

Two points came up repeatedly without anyone proposing them. First: nobody knows for certain what the future will look like, but that isn't enough of an excuse not to act. Second: we need to keep connecting and educating, because a huge part of the work ahead is getting this conversation to more people.

Sustainability can't be a topic you deal with once a year. It has to be on the table all the time.
