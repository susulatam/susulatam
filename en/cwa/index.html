The night

Fewer panels, more cheers.

SUSU was born to bring together people from industries that almost never cross paths. The after-party is exactly that, nighttime edition: no stage, no presentations, and nobody selling anything.

🃏

A short game so that, before the first toast, you've already met someone new.

🍻

Alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, something good to snack on, and background music low enough to talk.
